# Required Hero Background Images

Place the following images in this directory (`/public/assets/images/`):

## 1. Main Hero Image
- **File:** `hero.jpg`
- **Purpose:** Hero 1 background (main landing section)
- **Requirements:**
  - Format: JPG
  - Size: 1920×1080px (16:9 aspect ratio)
  - High quality, optimized for web (< 1MB)
  - Should represent the event theme and Black mental wellness

## 2. About Section Hero
- **File:** `about-hero.jpg`
- **Purpose:** Hero 2 background ("About the Conference" section)
- **Concept:** Warm community or networking environment in Ottawa
- **Style:**
  - Soft-focus background
  - Gold/brown tones (#CDA349, #4B2E05)
  - Professional but welcoming atmosphere
  - People connecting, not identifiable faces

## 3. Conference Schedule Hero
- **File:** `conference-hero.jpg`
- **Purpose:** Hero 3 background ("Conference Schedule" section)
- **Concept:** Indoor professional event setting
- **Style:**
  - People listening/attending (not clearly identifiable)
  - Gold/brown tone palette
  - Professional conference atmosphere
  - Warm, inclusive feeling

## 4. Waiting Section Hero
- **File:** `waiting-hero.jpg`
- **Purpose:** Hero 4 background ("We Are Waiting You!" section)
- **Concept:** Inclusive, warm welcome atmosphere
- **Style:**
  - People waving, greeting, or standing together
  - No identifiable faces
  - Gold and dark brown tones
  - Cinematic style
  - Welcoming, inviting mood

## Image Optimization Tips

1. Use tools like TinyPNG or ImageOptim to compress images
2. Maintain high quality while reducing file size
3. Ensure proper aspect ratios (16:9 recommended)
4. Test on different devices to ensure proper rendering
5. All images should have warm, gold/brown color grading

## Color Palette Reference

- **Deep Brown:** #4B2E05
- **Gold:** #CFA349
- **Black:** #000000
- **White:** #FFFFFF
